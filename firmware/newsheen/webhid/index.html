Motoko-S3 firmware newsheen-motoko-hid presents a composite CDC + vendor HID (usage page 0xFF00). Reports are 64 bytes of SLIP, padded with 0xC0. TYPE_PKT_RECV(0x03) and TYPE_EAPOL(0x06) frames carry a 12‑byte metadata prefix + the full 802.11 frame.
